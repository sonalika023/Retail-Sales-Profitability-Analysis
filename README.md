# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This portfolio project showcases data analysis and business problem-solving skills using Microsoft Excel. The project focuses on analyzing retail sales and profitability data to understand why high sales were not always converting into healthy profits.

---

## Data

Retail Sales Dataset (Superstore-like transactional dataset containing sales, profit, customer, product, region, and discount information).

---

## Problem Statement

The objective of this project was to analyze retail sales and profitability data to identify loss-making products, inefficient regions, and the impact of discounting on business performance. The analysis aimed to uncover the root causes behind weak profitability and provide data-driven recommendations to improve overall business performance.

---

## Analysis

Key business metrics were analyzed to understand sales and profitability performance. Specifically, the following KPIs were created:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin %
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value
* Average Profit Per Order
* Loss-Making Orders %

Various aspects of the dataset were analyzed and visualized to identify trends and business issues:

* Monthly Sales & Profit Trend Analysis
* Regional Sales & Profitability Analysis
* Category & Sub-Category Profitability Analysis
* Segment-wise Performance Analysis
* Discount vs Profit Analysis
* Top Profit-Making Products Analysis
* Loss-Making Product Analysis
* Root Cause Investigation for Profit Erosion

---

## Key Findings

* Tables and Bookcases were major loss-making sub-categories despite generating good sales.
* Central region generated higher sales than South region but showed weaker profitability.
* Discounts above 30% significantly reduced profitability.
* Extreme discounts between 50%–80% created major losses without enough revenue benefit.
* Phones, Copiers, and Paper emerged as strong profit-generating sub-categories.

---

## Root Cause Summary

The analysis showed that profitability issues were mainly caused by excessive discounting in certain sub-categories and regions. Loss-making sub-categories like Tables and Bookcases were associated with relatively higher discounts, especially in the Central region. Further analysis revealed that profits started declining sharply beyond 30% discount levels, making aggressive discounting one of the main drivers of profit erosion.

---

## Recommendations

* Control excessive discounting on low-margin products.
* Review pricing strategies for loss-making sub-categories.
* Focus more on profitable product categories and regions.
* Monitor loss-making orders regularly using dashboards and KPIs.
